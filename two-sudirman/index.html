335 Private Residences·70 Lantai·Handover Q1 2029

Two Sudirman Private Residences is a 70-floor, 335-unit ultra-luxury residential tower at Jl. Jend. Sudirman Kav. 2, Jakarta Pusat 10220. Developed by PT Central Sudirman Development — a joint venture of Mitsubishi Estate (Japan), Benhil Property, and Taspen Properti. Architecture by NBBJ (USA), structure by ARUP (UK), interior by Super Potato (Japan), façade by Inhabit (Singapore), landscape by TROP (Thailand), mechanical by Surbana Jurong (Singapore). Unit types: 2BR 175–192 sqm, 3BR 256–277 sqm, 4BR 308–320 sqm, Sky Terrace 405–651 sqm, Penthouse 708–2,345 sqm. Low Zone (FL 6–25) from IDR 71 million/sqm excl VAT. Handover Q1 2029. Strata title.
