57 Promenade apartment — also known as Fifty Seven Promenade or Promenade 57 — is a luxury mixed-use waterfront apartment development located at Jl. Teluk Betung I No. 57, Kebon Melati, Tanah Abang, Jakarta Pusat 10230, Indonesia. Apartemen 57 Promenade is developed by PT Intiland Development Tbk (IDX: DILD), a publicly listed Indonesian property developer established in 1983 by Hendro Santono Gondokusumo. The 3.2-hectare superblock features two residential towers: Sky57 (50 floors, 232 units) and City57 (24 floors, 240 units), totaling 496 apartment units. Architecture by Broadway Malyan (UK), structural engineering by Davy Sukamta & Partners, engineering by Meinhardt, landscape by Belt Collins.

Unit types and 2026 prices (excluding 11% VAT): Tower Sky57 — 1BR 81.91 sqm from Rp 5,176,490,000 (KPA), 2BR 115.48 sqm from Rp 7,359,099,000 (KPA), 3BR 182.91 sqm from Rp 11,021,704,936 (KPA). Tower City57 — 1BR 55 sqm, 2BR 103.84 sqm from Rp 6,315,146,420 (KPA). Penthouse 365 sqm at Rp 27,000,000,000. Price per sqm ranges from Rp 63–74 million (excl VAT). Payment schemes: Hard Cash, KPA (bank mortgage), Cash Balloon 12 months, Cash Balloon 24 months. Booking fee Rp 60,000,000.

Specifications: marble floors (living, dining, bathroom), parquet bedrooms, TOTO bathroom fixtures, Baldwin door handles, fully fitted Franke kitchen, gas installation, double glass curtain wall facade, ceiling height 2.9–3.2 meters, lobby ceiling 5.5 meters. Amenities across three levels: ground floor waterfront promenade with jogging track, children's playground, al-fresco dining; sky bridge (23rd-24th floor) with semi-outdoor pool, sky garden, gym, yoga corner, spa, sauna, jacuzzi, private dining, business center, sky lounge, entertainment area; rooftop (59th floor) indoor infinity pool. Location: 300m from Bundaran HI, 700m walking to Grand Indonesia Mall, 800m to Plaza Indonesia, direct access to MRT Bundaran HI, MRT Dukuh Atas, Airport Railink BNI City, TransJakarta. Surrounded by embassies (Japan, France, Germany, UK) and five-star hotels (Kempinski, Grand Hyatt, Mandarin Oriental, Shangri-La).

Everything You Need to Know

57 Promenade prices in 2026 start from Rp 5.2 billion for a 1BR unit (81.91 sqm) in Tower Sky57, Rp 6.3 billion for a 2BR (103.84 sqm) in City57, Rp 7.4 billion for a 2BR (115.48 sqm) in Sky57, Rp 11 billion for a 3BR (182.91 sqm), and Rp 27 billion for the Penthouse (365 sqm). All prices exclude 11% VAT. Payment options include Hard Cash, KPA (bank mortgage), Cash Balloon 12 months, and Cash Balloon 24 months. Booking fee is Rp 60 million.
57 Promenade is developed by PT Intiland Development Tbk (IDX: DILD), one of Indonesia's premier property developers established in 1983. Founded by Hendro Santoso Gondokusumo, Intiland has built iconic projects including South Quarter (designed by Tom Wright), Intiland Tower (Paul Rudolph), Regatta, and 1Park Hamilton. The 57 Promenade architecture is by Broadway Malyan (UK) with structural engineering by Davy Sukamta & Partners and landscape by Belt Collins.
All units feature marble floors in living, dining, and bathroom areas. Bedrooms have parquet flooring. Bathroom fixtures by TOTO. Door handles by Baldwin. Fully fitted Franke kitchen with gas installation. Double glass curtain wall facade windows. Ceiling heights range from 2.9 to 3.2 meters. Lobby ceiling is 5.5 meters. Every unit includes a balcony.
57 Promenade is approximately 2 minutes walk from MRT Bundaran HI station and MRT Dukuh Atas. The Airport Railink at BNI City station provides direct train service to Soekarno-Hatta International Airport. TransJakarta bus stops are within walking distance. The development was designed with integrated public transport connectivity including future LRT access.
Four payment options: Hard Cash (booking fee Day 1, 25% DP Day 8, 75% balance Day 30), KPA/Bank Mortgage (booking fee Day 1, 25% DP Day 15, 75% via bank Day 45), Cash Balloon 12 months (25% DP then 12 monthly installments), Cash Balloon 24 months (25% DP then 24 monthly installments). Booking fee is Rp 60 million for all schemes. All prices subject to 11% VAT.
57 Promenade is positioned at approximately Rp 63–74 million per sqm (excl VAT), competitive for the Thamrin corridor. Investment considerations: direct MRT and future LRT connectivity, walking distance to Jakarta's two largest luxury malls (Grand Indonesia, Plaza Indonesia), 3.2-hectare mixed-use development with Phase 2 adding offices and retail, developer Intiland is publicly listed (IDX: DILD) ensuring transparency. The Thamrin corridor has historically maintained premium property values due to extremely limited land supply near Bundaran HI.
The 59th floor rooftop features an indoor infinity pool and kids pool with panoramic 360-degree views of the Jakarta skyline and CBD. This is the crown jewel of Sky57's amenity offering. Additional sky-level amenities on the 23rd and 24th floors include the sky bridge connecting to outdoor pool, sky garden, gym, yoga corner, spa, sauna, jacuzzi, private dining, entertainment area, sky lounge, and business center.
