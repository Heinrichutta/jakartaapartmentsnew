Branz Mega Kuningan is a 482-unit Japanese-engineered luxury condominium at Jl. Perintis and Jl. Guru Mughni, Mega Kuningan, Jakarta Selatan 12950 — developed by PT Tokyu Land Indonesia (Tokyu Fudosan Holdings, Japan, in Indonesia since 1975). Construction by Shimizu Corporation (Japan, founded 1804). Architectural designer: Anggara Architeam. Interior designer: Field Four Design Office (Japan). Facade designer: Meinhardt. Landscape architect: Townland. M&E consultant: PT Arnan Pratama Consultants. Structural consultant: Navy Suarnata & Partners. Property management by Tokyu Property Management Indonesia (~822,000 units managed in Japan). Partially funded by JOIN (Japan Overseas Infrastructure Investment Corporation, 6.5 billion yen / ~850 billion IDR). Two towers on 1 hectare, 88 floors including Technical Floor 44. Unit types: Studio (Low Zone 12-17F), 1BR-A ±63/52 sqm (Low Zone), 1BR-C 1BR+1 ±80/64 sqm (Low+Mid Zone), 2BR-A ±74/60 sqm (all zones), 2BR-B ±80/64 sqm (High Zone 37-88F only), 3BR ±136/110 sqm (High+Mid Zone) — largest layout. Low Zone: 26 units per floor. Mid/High Zone: 14 units per floor. Handover commenced 2 December 2024 — ready to occupy. Over 200 units handed over; sales above 80%. Pet-friendly. Free PPN 12% on selected units. Ground-floor retail operational: Kem Chicks, Burma, Annathaya Wellness. Complex includes Swissotel Living Jakarta Mega Kuningan (Accor, 240 serviced apartments, opened April 2025). Five-layer security: GF manual check → Sky Lobby manual check → Sky Lobby access card → elevator access card → apartment dimple key. According to Colliers Indonesia Q4 2024, Jakarta CBD strata luxury absorption continues to skew toward ready-stock premium inventory. Marketing gallery: Noble House Building, 33rd Floor, Jl. Dr. Ide Anak Agung Gde Agung Kav. E.4.2 No. 2. Contact 0817803303 · info@jakartaapartments.co

IS BRANZ MEGA KUNINGAN PET-FRIENDLY?

Yes — One of Only Two in the Kuningan Corridor.

Branz Mega Kuningan is among the few Jakarta CBD luxury buildings that formally permit cats and dogs within resident policies. The Water Forest sky-deck includes soft-landscape zones appropriate for pets. Professional Japanese property management enforces quiet hours, hygiene, and common-area rules — the building stays premium for everyone.

In the Kuningan luxury corridor, only Verde Two and Branz Mega Kuningan pass the pet-policy filter among new luxury inventory.

AREA GUIDE

Why Mega Kuningan Is Different From the Rest of Jakarta CBD.

Mega Kuningan sits inside Jakarta's Golden Triangle CBD — bounded by Jl. HR Rasuna Said, Jl. Gatot Subroto, and Jl. Prof. Dr. Satrio. Home to the largest concentration of foreign embassies in Jakarta, multiple five-star hotel brands, international headquarters, and one of the densest clusters of Grade-A office inventory in Indonesia.
