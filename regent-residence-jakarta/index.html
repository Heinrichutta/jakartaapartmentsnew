What You Should Know

Regent Residences Jakarta prices start from IDR 6.6 Billion for the 2 Bedroom unit (99/101 sqm). The 3 Bedroom (251 sqm), furnished by Magran Living, is priced at IDR 21 Billion. The Penthouse (536 sqm) is IDR 39 Billion. The Grand Penthouse (1014 sqm with private pool, 1 unit remaining) is priced upon request.
Regent Residences Jakarta is developed by KG Global Developments and managed by Regent Hotels & Resorts under Intercontinental Hotels Group (IHG). It is one of only 3 Regent Branded Residences globally, making it an exceptionally rare asset class.
All Regent Residences Jakarta owners receive IHG Diamond Elite membership from day one. This is the highest tier of IHG's loyalty program — unlocking room upgrades, executive lounge access, late checkout, and priority services at IHG properties worldwide, both at home and while traveling.
Regent Residences Jakarta stands at 57 storeys in the Jakarta Golden Triangle. The tower features private pools in select penthouse units, double private lifts, and full Intercontinental-standard hospitality management for all residents.
Four unit types are available: Grand Penthouse (1014 sqm with private pool, price upon request, 1 unit remaining), Penthouse (536 sqm, IDR 39 Billion), 3 Bedroom (251 sqm, furnished by Magran Living, IDR 21 Billion), and 2 Bedroom (99/101 sqm, IDR 6.6 Billion).
The 3 Bedroom show unit at Regent Residences Jakarta is furnished by Magran Living, a luxury interior furnishing company. Select units also feature furniture by MOIE. The Grand Penthouse and Penthouse units are designed for bespoke customization by the owner.
Regent Residences Jakarta is located in the Jakarta Golden Triangle — the premium CBD corridor encompassing Sudirman, Kuningan, and Thamrin. This is Jakarta's most prestigious address corridor, home to the city's leading financial institutions, embassies, and luxury developments.
There are only 3 Regent Branded Residences globally. Regent Residences Jakarta is one of them. This extreme rarity positions it not just as a luxury apartment but as a collectible residential asset managed by Regent Hotels & Resorts under Intercontinental Hotels Group.
