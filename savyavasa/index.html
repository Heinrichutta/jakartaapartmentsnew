ii. The Difference Editorial · Declarative
WHAT MAKES SAVYAVASA SAVYAVASA

Four things this property holds by footprint.

Savyavasa sits on three hectares of land in one of the city's two most established neighbourhoods, held by two developers with fifty years of global residential work between them. Here is what that produces.

01.

The First Green Mark Gold in Jakarta

Savyavasa is the first residential project in Jakarta to achieve Green Mark Gold — Singapore BCA's highest-level sustainability standard. This is not a plaque on the wall. It is a building designed, built, and operated to measurable environmental performance.

02.

Three Hectares. 230+ Trees. One Address.

An urban forest sits on the site — not adjacent to it, not visible from it, but inside it. A 1-kilometre jogging trail wraps around natural parkland. The pathways you walk through were designed by Intaran Design, the Bali studio behind Indonesia's most considered landscape work.

03.

Air That Is Filtered Before It Reaches You

Indoor air is passed through PM 2.5 filtration at the source. You do not need an air purifier in the living room. The tap water is treated to drinkable standard. The details that typically belong to a Singapore or Hong Kong tower are here, in Jakarta, by default.

04.

The First AI-Integrated Residence in Indonesia

Through a partnership with Habitap, Savyavasa became Indonesia's first AI-integrated residence. Control climate, lighting, curtains, facility bookings, visitor access, and package delivery through one application. Voice control integrated. Connection through WhatsApp for facility reservation.

THE GREEN COMPOUND

Not a building with a garden. A forest that happens to contain three towers.

At Savyavasa, the ratio of green area to built area is among the highest of any residential project in South Jakarta — and it was achieved by designing the landscape first. Every path, every tree, every watering line was planned before the towers were located. This is why the air here feels different. And why the neighbours you pass have dogs. Below are the nine things that quietly define life inside the compound.

01 · URBAN FOREST

Three hectares. 230+ trees. One kilometre of trail.

The site spans 3 hectares of landscape. More than 230 mature trees populate the compound — not decorative potted plants, but real canopy cover. A 1-kilometre jogging trail wraps around the natural parkland, and the urban forest is directly accessible from the ground floor of each tower. Intaran Design, the Bali-based landscape studio, specified every tree by species and placement.

02 · PET FRIENDLY

A compound built around the reality of owning a dog.

Savyavasa is pet friendly by policy and by design. Internal pet walking paths, low-traffic private roads, and shaded tree corridors make the 1km trail practical for daily dog walks. On-site pet care services are available through the compound, reducing the need to travel outside Dharmawangsa for routine needs. The compound was built for residents whose animals belong to the family.

03 · PM 2.5 FILTERED AIR

The air purifier is already installed. Inside every AC.

Air quality matters. Savyavasa's response sits at the building level, not the occupant level: indoor air is passed through PM 2.5 filtration integrated at source. Residents do not need to purchase separate air purifiers for the living room, bedroom, or study. The specification is particularly relevant for families with young children, elderly residents, or anyone with respiratory sensitivity.

04 · DRINKABLE TAP WATER

Water from the tap. Not from the bottle.

Savyavasa's water system is specified to produce drinkable tap water throughout the residence — kitchen, bathroom, and all access points. This matches the residential water standard used in Singapore, Tokyo, and Hong Kong. The daily cost of bottled water, and the waste produced, disappears.

05 · AMPLE PARKING

Two to four cars per unit. Private, covered, assigned.

Parking allocation at Savyavasa is designed for households with multiple vehicles. Multiple assigned private parking bays per unit, sufficient for households with multiple drivers or families maintaining more than one vehicle. All parking is covered and indoor. EV charging stations are available within the compound — Savyavasa was specified for electric vehicle infrastructure from the masterplan stage.

06 · NATURAL VENTILATION

Windows that open. Balconies that breathe.

Every unit is specified for natural cross-ventilation. The long balconies were designed not as architectural statement but as working outdoor space — to allow for indoor-outdoor airflow throughout the day. Double-glazed low-e windows reduce heat gain and sound intrusion while still opening fully. The 4BR and larger units feature double-height living and dining areas for volumetric air movement.

07 · GREEN MARK GOLD

Jakarta's first residential certification at this grade.

Savyavasa holds Green Mark Gold certification from the Building and Construction Authority (BCA) of Singapore — the first Jakarta residential project to do so. Green Mark Gold evaluates energy performance, water conservation, material sustainability, and indoor environmental quality. The building is also specified for LED and T5 lighting, composting infrastructure, water recycling, and soil moisture sensors that maintain the urban forest without over-watering.

08 · HABITAP SMART HOME

The first AI-integrated residence in Indonesia.

Through partnership with Habitap, Savyavasa is Indonesia's first AI-integrated residence. One application controls climate, lighting, curtains, kitchen appliances, door access, package delivery notification, and visitor approval. Facility booking (pool, gym, Hammam, tennis court, multifunction room) is done through the application or via WhatsApp-linked reservation. Voice activation is integrated. License plate recognition opens the parking barrier automatically.

09 · THE KIDS CARE ECOSYSTEM

An indoor playroom built on Howard Gardner's 9 Intelligences.

Savyavasa's Kids Playroom was designed around the Howard Gardner model of multiple intelligences — not as a marketing line, but as the actual structural principle. A stage for storytelling, a Kids Bank for role-play, Lego tables for logic, art corners, a kids gym with climbing wall, musical instruments, a Mini City for collaborative play, and nature-inspired settings. Plus a dedicated Kids Care facility for supervised childcare.
